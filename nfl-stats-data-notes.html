How we calculate NFL Stats

What is counted, how a cover is decided, and how to tell us about an error.

Updated Sep 25, 2026

What is counted

  • Every regular-season NFL game since 2021 that has a final score and a point spread. Playoffs and preseason games are not included.
  • Each game's point spread and final score, as recorded for that game.
  • A team's record is counted from that team's own point of view: home and away, favorite and underdog.

How ATS is calculated

  • Cover margin = the team's winning margin minus the spread it had to beat. A positive cover margin is a cover; negative is a non-cover.
  • Example: a 3-point underdog (+3) that loses by 2 has a cover margin of +1, so it covers. A 3-point favorite (-3) that wins by 3 lands exactly on the spread, which is a push.
  • Pushes are shown in records (for example 9-8-1) and left out of cover percentages. Cover % = covers divided by covers plus non-covers.
  • A game with no spread (a pick'em) has no favorite or underdog and is left out of favorite and underdog splits.

How over/under is calculated

  • A game goes over if the two teams' combined score is higher than the posted total, under if lower, and is a push if equal. Records read over-under-push.
  • Over % excludes pushes. Weather splits use the game-time temperature and wind recorded for outdoor games only.

Definitions used on the stats pages

  • Thursday, Monday and Sunday night games: Thursday games starting at 7:00 PM ET or later, all Monday games, and Sunday games starting at 7:00 PM ET or later.
  • Teams off a bye: 13 or more days of rest before the game (Week 1 excluded). Short rest: 5 days or fewer.
  • Division games: both teams in the same division. Big spreads: 7 or more points, or 10 or more where labeled.

Spreads and corrections

  • Spreads come from a single recorded line for each game. If we find a recorded line that differs from the market at the time, we correct it and use the corrected number everywhere on the site, including the Track Record.
  • The date under each page's headline is when its numbers were last refreshed. Numbers refresh with the site's daily update during the season.
  • Found something wrong? Email help@trifectapicks.com with the page and the game and we will look at it.
