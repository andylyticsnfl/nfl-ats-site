Questions

What does ATS mean?

ATS means against the spread. A team covers when it wins by more than the point spread, or loses by fewer points than the spread. A game that lands exactly on the spread is a push and is left out of cover percentages.

What is cover percentage?

Cover percentage is the share of games a team covers the spread, out of games that were not a push: covers divided by covers plus non-covers.

How often are the NFL stats updated?

Standings, results, trends and game pages refresh with the site's daily update during the season. The date under each headline shows the latest update.
