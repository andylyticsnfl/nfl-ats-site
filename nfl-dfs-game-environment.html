How to read game environment

Game environment is the market's picture of a game before kickoff: how many points it expects in total (the game total), who it expects to lead (the spread), and how those points split between the teams (implied points). DFS player props on stats like passing yards, rushing yards, receptions and touchdowns tend to follow how much a team is expected to score and how the game is expected to unfold. This page shows the environment for every game. It is not a prediction or a pick, and it does not account for injuries, matchups or player roles, which matter a lot for individual props.

Questions

What is game environment in NFL DFS?

Game environment is the market's picture of a game: the posted total (expected combined points), the point spread, and the implied points each team is expected to score. It is a starting point for judging how much scoring and volume a game may produce.

How do you calculate implied team points?

The favorite's implied points are (total + spread) / 2 and the underdog's are (total - spread) / 2. A game with a total of 46 and a 4-point spread implies 25 points for the favorite and 21 for the underdog.

Does a high game total mean a shootout?

Not always. Since 2021, games posted at 47 or more reached 50 points 48% of the time.
