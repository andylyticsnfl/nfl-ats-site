METHODOLOGY

How the model actually works

Not a black box, and not a "secret formula" pitch. Here's the real discipline behind every pick on this site.

01

Zero market data as a training input

Spreads, moneylines, and line movement are never fed into the model that generates a prediction. The model forms an independent view of the game first. The closing line only enters the picture afterward, to define what "covered" means for grading and as a benchmark to compare against — never as something the model learns from.

02

Point-in-time correctness, enforced mechanically

Every statistic that feeds a prediction is exactly what was knowable before that game kicked off — nothing from later in the season leaks backward. This isn't a promise; it's checked automatically on every build with an independent leakage-detection pass.

03

Walk-forward validation, not curve-fitting

The model is tested the way it would actually be used: trained only on the past, asked to predict a season it has never seen, one week at a time. Numbers that only work with the benefit of hindsight don't make it onto this site.

04

Confidence, ranked honestly

Every week, each game is ranked by how far the model's own view diverges from the market's. The single largest divergence is the Top 1 pick; the five largest make up the Top 5. Those tiers are fixed in advance by the model's own math — never chosen after the results are in.

05

Full-slate transparency

Every graded pick from 2021 through 2025 is on the Track Record page — the wins and the losses. Nothing is filtered out after the fact to make a number look better.

06

Built on public, verifiable data

Team and player performance data, efficiency metrics, injury reports, roster continuity, and situational tendencies — all from publicly available NFL data sources, refreshed continuously through the season.

What this isn't

No guaranteed win rate, no "lock of the week," no claim that any single pick is a sure thing. Sports outcomes are inherently uncertain — what's on this site is a rigorously backtested edge, shown with its full history, not a promise.
