Zero market data, by design

The model that produces these picks never sees a spread, a moneyline, or a bet split during training. It's built entirely from team and player performance data — then, only afterward, checked against the closing line to see whether it actually found something the market missed.

Point-in-time, mechanically enforced

Every input is what was actually knowable before kickoff — no accidental use of information from later in the season. This is checked automatically on every model run, not just assumed.

Backtested on 5 years, not cherry-picked

Every number on this site comes from a walk-forward test across the 2021–2025 seasons — the model never saw a season's outcomes before being asked to predict it.

Full slate, graded in public

No hiding behind a "best bets" filter that changes after the fact. The full pick log — every game, every week, right or wrong — is on the Track Record page.

Confidence tiers, not vibes

Every week the model ranks its own picks by how large its disagreement with the market is. The top 5 and top 1 by that measure are tracked separately, exactly as shown here.
