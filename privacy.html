Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Trifecta ("we," "us," "our") collects when you use trifectapicks.com and our subscription service, and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the email address you provide when subscribing or signing in, the name you provide at checkout, and basic usage data from visiting the site. Payment details (card numbers, etc.) are collected and processed entirely by our payment processor, Whop — we never see or store your full payment information.

2. How We Use Information

We use your email address to deliver picks, send Encyclopedia sign-in ("magic") links, and respond to support requests. We don't use your information for anything beyond operating and improving the Service.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of third parties to operate the Service:

  • Whop — payment processing and subscription management.
  • Brevo — delivery of transactional emails (picks, magic links, receipts).
  • Cloudflare — hosting for our API, DNS, and privacy-friendly site analytics.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting for the public website.

Each of these providers only receives the information needed to perform its function and has its own privacy practices governing that data.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless — it doesn't set tracking cookies or build a cross-site profile of you. It gives us aggregate traffic counts only.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above, as needed to operate the Service, or if required by law.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your subscription is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for records and support purposes, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing help@trifectapicks.com.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at, and may not be used by, anyone under 21.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email help@trifectapicks.com.
